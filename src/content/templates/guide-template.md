## Guide Structure

### 1. Quick Overview (100-150 words)

- What this guide covers
- Who it's for
- Time commitment required
- What you'll need (worksheets, apps, etc.)

### 2. Before You Begin (200-300 words)

- Prerequisites or mental preparation
- When to use this technique
- When NOT to use it (safety considerations)
- Setting realistic expectations

### 3. Step-by-Step Process

#### Step 1: [Action Name] (200-300 words)

- **What to do**: Clear, specific instructions
- **Why it works**: Brief therapeutic rationale
- **Example**: Concrete example or scenario
- **Common mistakes**: What to avoid
- **Time needed**: Realistic timeframe

#### Step 2: [Action Name] (200-300 words)

[Same format as Step 1]

#### Step 3: [Action Name] (200-300 words)

[Same format as Step 1]

_Continue for 5-7 steps total_

### 4. Troubleshooting Common Challenges

- **Challenge 1**: [Problem] → [Solution]
- **Challenge 2**: [Problem] → [Solution]
- **Challenge 3**: [Problem] → [Solution]

### 5. Practice and Integration (200-300 words)

- How often to practice
- Ways to remember to use the technique
- Signs that it's working
- How to modify for your situation

### 6. Next Steps and Advanced Techniques (150-200 words)

- What to do once you've mastered the basics
- Related techniques to explore
- When to seek professional guidance

## Special Elements for Guides

### Interactive Components

- Checklists for each step
- Progress tracking suggestions
- Worksheet or template downloads
- Practice schedule templates

### Visual Aids

- Flowcharts for decision points
- Infographics for complex processes
- Before/after comparison charts
- Progress tracking visuals

### Safety Considerations

- Clear warnings about when to stop
- Crisis resources readily available
- Professional help recommendations
- Contraindications or risk factors

## Writing Guidelines for Guides

### Structure

- **Sequential**: Each step builds on the previous
- **Modular**: Steps can be practiced independently
- **Flexible**: Adaptable to different situations
- **Progressive**: Starts simple, becomes more nuanced

### Language

- **Imperative**: Use action verbs and direct instructions
- **Specific**: Avoid vague language like "try to" or "maybe"
- **Encouraging**: Acknowledge difficulty while building confidence
- **Personal**: Use "you" to create connection

### Testing

- **Walkthrough**: Follow your own instructions step-by-step
- **Timing**: Verify realistic time estimates
- **Accessibility**: Test with different skill levels
- **Safety**: Ensure no harmful misinterpretations

## Quality Checklist

Before publishing, verify:

- [ ] Each step has clear, actionable instructions
- [ ] Steps are in logical, sequential order
- [ ] Time estimates are realistic
- [ ] Common challenges are addressed
- [ ] Safety considerations are prominent
- [ ] Examples are relatable and diverse
- [ ] Professional help guidelines are clear
- [ ] Practice recommendations are specific
- [ ] Progress indicators are defined
- [ ] Next steps are outlined
