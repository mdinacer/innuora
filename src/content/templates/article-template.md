This template provides a structure for creating high-quality therapeutic content that aligns with Innuora's CBT-based approach.

## Article Structure

### 1. Introduction (150-200 words)

- Hook the reader with a relatable scenario or statistic
- Briefly acknowledge the challenge they're facing
- Promise specific, actionable solutions
- Preview what they'll learn

### 2. Understanding the Problem (300-500 words)

- Explain the psychological/therapeutic context
- Use CBT principles to frame the issue
- Include relevant research or clinical insights
- Help readers identify with the experience

### 3. Core Content Sections (1000-1500 words total)

Organize into 3-5 main sections, each with:

- Clear subheading
- Practical techniques or insights
- Step-by-step instructions where applicable
- Real-world examples (anonymized)

#### Example Sections:

- **Technique 1: [Specific Method]**
- **Technique 2: [Specific Method]**
- **Common Challenges and Solutions**
- **When to Seek Professional Help**

### 4. Practical Application (200-300 words)

- Summarize key takeaways
- Provide a simple exercise or worksheet
- Suggest implementation timeline
- Link to relevant Innuora features

### 5. Resources and Next Steps (100-150 words)

- Related articles within Innuora content library
- Professional resources if needed
- Encouragement for continued progress

## Writing Guidelines

### Tone and Voice

- **Compassionate**: Acknowledge struggle without minimizing
- **Empowering**: Focus on reader's capacity for change
- **Evidence-based**: Reference CBT principles and research
- **Practical**: Always include actionable steps

### Content Quality Standards

- **Clinical accuracy**: All techniques should be evidence-based
- **Accessibility**: Write at 8th-grade reading level
- **Cultural sensitivity**: Avoid assumptions about background/experience
- **Safety-first**: Include crisis resources when appropriate

### SEO Optimization

- Use primary keyword in title, first paragraph, and at least one subheading
- Include semantic keywords naturally throughout
- Optimize for featured snippets with numbered lists and clear answers
- Link to related internal content

### CBT Integration

- Connect content to specific therapeutic modules
- Include thought records or behavioral experiments where relevant
- Reference cognitive distortions when applicable
- Provide homework or practice suggestions

## Content Checklist

Before publishing, ensure:

- [ ] Title is compelling and SEO-optimized
- [ ] Introduction hooks the reader and sets clear expectations
- [ ] Content includes at least 3 actionable techniques
- [ ] Each technique has step-by-step instructions
- [ ] Clinical accuracy has been verified
- [ ] Reading level is appropriate (8th grade or lower)
- [ ] Crisis resources are included when relevant
- [ ] Related content links are added
- [ ] Meta description is optimized
- [ ] Keywords are naturally integrated
