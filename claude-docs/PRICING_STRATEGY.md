## Executive Summary

Innuora's pricing is **value-based, not cost-based**. We charge for the clinical value delivered (professional-grade psychological diagnostics), not just API token consumption. This document explains the pricing structure, rationale, and market positioning.

## Pricing Rationale

### Why Value-Based Pricing?

**We DO NOT charge based on:**

- ❌ API token costs (~$0.015 per diagnostic)
- ❌ Infrastructure overhead
- ❌ "Fair markup" on costs

**We DO charge based on:**

- ✅ Perceived clinical value to user
- ✅ Comparable professional services pricing
- ✅ Market positioning vs competitors
- ✅ What users are WILLING to pay for outcomes

### Value Justification by Tier

**FREE ($0):**

- No cost to try
- Demonstrates system quality
- Creates upgrade motivation

**STARTER ($35):**

- Less than: Therapy book ($25-50), meditation app subscription ($70/year)
- Comparable to: One-time self-help resource
- Value: Personalized insights (not generic advice)

**REGULAR ($75):**

- Less than: Therapy session ($150-300), therapy apps ($70-100/year)
- Comparable to: Monthly wellness subscription, gym membership
- Value: Structured CBT system + continuous support

**PREMIUM ($150):**

- Less than: Professional assessment ($500-1500 one-time), monthly therapy ($300+)
- Comparable to: Therapy platform (Modern Health, Lyra)
- Value: **Unlimited $500 assessments** + therapist collaboration

## Revenue Model

### Unit Economics

**Average Revenue Per User (ARPU):**

- Assuming 50% Free, 30% Starter, 15% Regular, 5% Premium
- ARPU = (0.50 × $0) + (0.30 × $35) + (0.15 × $75) + (0.05 × $150)
- **ARPU ≈ $29.25/user/month**

**Cost Per User (CPU):**

- API costs: ~$0.40 per 100 messages
- Infrastructure: ~$2/user/month (hosting, database, CDN)
- Average user sends 150 messages + 5 diagnostics/month
- **CPU ≈ $2.70/user/month**

**Gross Margin:**

- ($29.25 - $2.70) / $29.25 = **90.8% gross margin**

### Credit Economics

**Credit Value:**

- 1 credit = $0.005 USD (half a cent)
- Consumer pays based on tier pricing
- Actual cost: ~$0.0015 per credit (API + infrastructure)
- **Margin: 70-90% depending on usage**

**Diagnostic Economics:**

- Basic diagnostic: ~650 tokens → ~1 credit cost → ~$0.005 charged
- Standard diagnostic: ~2848 tokens → ~3 credits cost → ~$0.015 charged
- Advanced diagnostic: ~3311 tokens → ~3 credits cost → ~$0.015 charged
- **Actual API cost: ~$0.005-0.015 per diagnostic**
- **Perceived value: $500-1500 (professional assessment)**

## Discount Strategy

### Current Policy: No Discounts

**Why no discounts:**

- Value-based pricing already positioned competitively
- Discounts reduce perceived value
- We're not competing on price (we're competing on clinical quality)

### Future Considerations (Post-Launch)

**Potential Discount Scenarios:**

1. **Annual billing**: 20% discount ($720 → $576 for Regular tier)

   - Improves cash flow
   - Reduces churn
   - Standard SaaS practice

2. **Student discount**: 30-40% off

   - Universities are a target market
   - Students have less disposable income
   - Future B2B opportunity

3. **Therapist referral**: Special rate for therapy clients

   - Therapists recommend to their clients
   - Word-of-mouth growth
   - Professional validation

4. **Non-profit partnerships**: Deep discount for underserved communities
   - Mission-driven pricing ($3-6/person)
   - PR and social impact
   - Separate from consumer pricing

**DO NOT DISCOUNT:**

- ❌ "Launch special" (devalues product)
- ❌ "Limited time offer" (creates urgency but damages brand)
- ❌ Black Friday / seasonal sales (positions as commodity)

## FAQ: Pricing Strategy

### Why not charge per diagnostic instead of credits?

**Answer:** Credits provide flexibility and transparency. Users can choose how to spend (more conversation OR more diagnostics). Fixed diagnostic pricing creates decision fatigue ("Should I generate now or wait?"). Credits feel like currency, diagnostics feel like transactions.

### Why is Premium only 2x Regular, not more?

**Answer:** Premium is about **access** (clinical diagnostic tier), not volume. The value is "unlimited $500 assessments," not "more credits." Pricing reflects therapist collaboration capability, not just credit quantity.

### Why not offer subscriptions instead of credit packages?

**Answer:** Currently testing one-time credit purchases to validate product-market fit. Subscriptions create churn anxiety and require billing infrastructure. Future consideration: hybrid model (subscription + top-up credits).

### How do we justify $150/month for Premium?

**Answer:**

1. Professional assessment worth $500-1500
2. Therapist collaboration (email diagnostics)
3. Unlimited clinical reports
4. Less than therapy platforms ($60-100/month for limited features)
5. Target market: people already in therapy ($300+/month)

### What prevents users from just using Free tier forever?

**Answer:** Free tier only shows `whats_happening` (basic patterns). After 12 messages, users naturally want to know WHY patterns exist, what hidden rules drive them, and HOW to change. Free tier creates awareness, paid tiers provide action.
