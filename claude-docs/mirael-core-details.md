# Mirael: Non-Clinical Positioning

## Why Mirael Is Not a Clinical App

### 1. No Diagnosis or Treatment

- Mirael **does not diagnose mental health conditions** (e.g., depression, anxiety disorders, PTSD).
- It **does not provide medical or psychological treatment**, prescriptions, or formal therapy.
- Interactions are **reflective and educational**, aimed at self-awareness, not clinical intervention.

### 2. Self-Reflection & Emotional Insight Focus

- Mirael’s purpose is **emotional clarity and pattern recognition**, not clinical therapy.
- Users explore thoughts, feelings, and internal pressures **without replacing professional care**.

### 3. Non-Clinical Methodology

- The app leverages **cognitive and behavioral frameworks** (CBT-informed reflection, pattern recognition) but **does not implement formal therapy protocols**.
- It avoids clinical tools such as psychological testing, symptom scoring, or diagnostic questionnaires.

### 4. Target Audience & Positioning

- Mirael is designed for **high-functioning women seeking self-understanding** and emotional clarity, not for individuals requiring clinical treatment.
- It’s positioned as a **personal development and emotional insight tool**, not a therapy platform.

### 5. Liability & Compliance

- Because it is **not clinical**, Mirael does **not fall under medical device or mental health regulation** in most jurisdictions.
- The app should **include disclaimers** clarifying that it is for self-reflection and personal insight, **not a replacement for professional care**.

## Messaging Points

- Mirael is an **“emotional clarity assistant”**.
- It is **self-help oriented**, not a clinical platform.
- All suggestions are **informational and reflective**, not prescriptive treatment.
- Users retain **full agency**-they are guided to insights, not interventions.
