See Mirael in Action

You: I have a big presentation tomorrow. I feel like if I mess up, it will be a disaster for me and my team.
Generic AI
It sounds like you're anxious. Maybe review your slides and practice; preparation can help reduce stress.
Mirael
I hear the weight you're carrying. There's a silent rule here: you must perform perfectly, or it's a disaster. That belief creates tension. What sensations do you notice in your body as you think about this?
You: My chest feels tight, I can't focus, and I keep imagining worst-case outcomes.
Visualizing worst-case scenarios can be stressful. Try breaking the presentation into steps and practicing each one.
That's catastrophizing — imagining disaster before it happens. Recognizing it is the first step. You're prepared, and preparation is within your control. Let's identify one tangible action you can take right now to ease pressure.
You: I can rehearse the key points and check the slides one more time.
Mirael: Perfect. Focusing on what's actionable reduces stress. Notice how this small step shifts your tension and perspective.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Mirael?

Mirael is an emotional mirror that helps you pause, reflect, and see your inner world clearly through short, emotionally attuned conversations.

How is Mirael different from therapy?

Mirael is not therapy and cannot replace professional care. It is a reflective companion designed to support awareness, not to diagnose or treat mental health conditions.

How does Mirael work?

Mirael uses conversational prompts to reflect emotions, highlight hidden pressures, recurring thoughts, and unspoken rules, giving insight and actionable steps.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. All reflections are private. You have full control, including the ability to export or delete conversations.

Who is Mirael for?

Mirael is built for people who often appear strong outwardly but feel emotionally drained inside. It provides a safe space to reflect, notice patterns, and regain clarity.

Can Mirael help in a crisis?

No. Mirael is not a crisis service. In immediate danger or emergencies, contact local authorities or professional hotlines.

How much does it cost?

Mirael is free to use initially. Pricing will be introduced as the platform evolves.

Why use Mirael?

Mirael helps you notice patterns, understand hidden pressures, and practice reflective awareness — turning stress into actionable insight.

How often should I use Mirael?

Short, regular sessions help you recognize patterns and practice conscious awareness over time.

Important: Mirael is not a mental health or crisis service. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services.
