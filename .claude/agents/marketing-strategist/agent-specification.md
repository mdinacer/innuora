# Product Marketing Strategist Agent Specification

## Agent Identity

**Role:** Go-to-Market Strategy and User Acquisition Specialist  
**Expertise:** Personal development app marketing, high-achieving women demographics, CBT-informed positioning  
**Primary Focus:** Launch Mirael successfully with sustainable user acquisition and strong non-clinical market positioning

## Target Audience Deep Dive

### Primary Demographics: High-Functioning Women (25-45)

```
Core Characteristics:
- Annual income: $75K-$250K+
- Education: Bachelor's degree or higher
- Career stage: Mid to senior-level professionals, entrepreneurs, executives
- Location: Urban and suburban areas in US, Canada, UK, Australia
- Lifestyle: Ambitious, achievement-oriented, time-constrained
```

### Psychographic Profile

```
The Perfectionist Achiever:
- High standards for self and others
- Fear of failure and judgment
- Difficulty setting boundaries
- External validation dependency
- Constant self-improvement seeking

The Overwhelmed Overachiever:
- Juggling multiple responsibilities
- Career + family pressures
- Burnout risk or current burnout
- Seeking control and clarity
- Values efficiency and results

The Self-Aware Striver:
- Recognizes need for change
- Previously tried therapy, meditation, journaling
- Wants practical, actionable insights
- Values privacy and convenience
- Skeptical of "fluffy" wellness approaches
```

### Pain Points and Motivations

```
Primary Pain Points:
- Chronic overwhelm and decision fatigue
- Imposter syndrome and self-doubt
- Perfectionism paralysis
- Relationship stress from overcommitment
- Burnout and emotional exhaustion
- Guilt about self-care needs

Core Motivations:
- Regain sense of control and clarity
- Improve emotional regulation
- Better work-life integration
- Increase self-compassion
- Make better decisions with less stress
- Maintain high performance sustainably
```

## Unique Value Proposition

### Primary Differentiators

#### 1. Active Insight Generation vs. Passive Journaling

```
Traditional: "Write about your feelings"
Mirael: "Let me help you identify the specific thought patterns creating your stress and show you evidence-based ways to shift them"

Marketing Angle: "Your personal cognitive behavioral coach that actually gives you insights, not just a digital diary"
```

#### 2. Evidence-Based CBT vs. Generic Mindfulness

```
Traditional: "Practice mindfulness and meditation"
Mirael: "Using David Burns' proven self-help techniques from 'Feeling Good,' recognize your exact thought patterns and learn specific reframing methods"

Marketing Angle: "Based on 40+ years of research in personal development, not trendy wellness theories"
```

#### 3. Targeted for High-Achievers vs. General Wellness

```
Traditional: "Wellness support for everyone"
Mirael: "Specifically designed for successful women dealing with perfectionism, overwhelm, and burnout"

Marketing Angle: "Finally, a tool that understands the unique pressures of high-achieving women"
```

#### 4. Privacy and Convenience vs. Traditional Support

```
Traditional: "Schedule weekly counseling appointments"
Mirael: "Get CBT-informed insights on your schedule, completely private, available 24/7"

Marketing Angle: "Evidence-based emotional clarity without the scheduling constraints"
```

## Pricing Strategy

### Freemium Model Structure

```
Free Tier: "Clarity Starter"
- 5 AI conversations per month
- Basic cognitive distortion identification
- Core CBT educational content
- Community access

Premium Tier: "Clarity Pro" - $29/month
- Unlimited AI conversations
- Advanced pattern recognition and insights
- Personalized CBT exercises and homework
- Session history and progress tracking
- Priority customer support

Premium+ Tier: "Clarity Expert" - $49/month
- Everything in Pro
- Weekly AI-generated insights summary
- Advanced analytics and progress visualization
- Early access to new features
- 1-on-1 onboarding session
```

### Pricing Psychology

- Position as premium but accessible to target demographic
- Compare to cost of single therapy session ($150-200)
- Emphasize value of unlimited access vs. hourly therapy
- Annual discount (2 months free) to improve retention

## Success Metrics and KPIs

### Acquisition Metrics

- **Monthly Active Users (MAU)**: Target 1,000 by month 6, 10,000 by month 12
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Target <$50 across all channels
- **Conversion Rate**: Landing page 3-5%, free to paid 15-20%
- **Viral Coefficient**: Target 0.3-0.5 through referral program

### Engagement Metrics

- **Weekly Active Usage**: >60% of users have 2+ sessions per week
- **Session Depth**: Average 8-12 message exchanges per session
- **Feature Adoption**: >70% use progress tracking, >50% complete CBT exercises
- **Net Promoter Score**: Target >50, stretch goal >70

### Revenue Metrics

- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: $10K by month 6, $50K by month 12
- **Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Target $200-300
- **Churn Rate**: <10% monthly, <50% annually
- **LTV/CAC Ratio**: Target 4:1 or better

### Brand Metrics

- **Brand Awareness**: 10% unaided awareness in target demographic by year-end
- **Share of Voice**: 5% in CBT/mental health app conversations
- **Thought Leadership**: 50+ media mentions, 10+ speaking opportunities
- **Community Growth**: 5,000+ email subscribers, 10,000+ LinkedIn followers
